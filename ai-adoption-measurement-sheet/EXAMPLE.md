# AI adoption measurement sheet: an example

*Illustrative example with sample data.* The company is an illustrative 70-person freight forwarder; its people, carriers and cases are illustrative too, and nothing here happened. Its quotes desk has four quoting coordinators and a desk lead. Since 14 September 2026 the desk has used a quote request preparation workflow: for each incoming request it prepares a draft quote from the customer's request and the carriers' rate sheets, and a coordinator checks and sends every quote. The desk lead owns the workflow. On Monday 2 November 2026 she prepares the sheet for October, the first full month of use. What she supplied comes first, in full. Then the sheet that came back.
